## PROJECT OVERVIEW

Eira Grove is an upcoming luxury hillside resort located in Kakkadampoyil, Kerala, India.

The resort is currently under construction and has not officially opened.

This website is not intended to be a traditional resort website.

The goal is to create a cinematic digital destination that allows visitors to emotionally experience the resort before it exists in reality.

The experience should feel closer to a luxury film, architectural showcase, and interactive story than a conventional hospitality website.

The attached architectural walkthrough video is the primary source of truth for the visual language, spatial layout, camera journey, terrain characteristics, architectural hierarchy, mood, and storytelling sequence.

The video must be continuously referenced throughout development.

# USER EXPERIENCE GOAL

When a visitor enters the website:

They should feel as if they are slowly travelling through the future resort.

The website should not immediately expose all content.

The environment should reveal itself gradually through cinematic storytelling.

The scroll wheel should behave like a camera controller.

The user should feel they are moving through the resort.

The experience should have a beginning, middle, climax, and conclusion.

# TERRAIN DESIGN

This is one of the most important parts of the project.

The terrain is not generic.

The terrain must not be randomly generated.

The terrain must not resemble a flat landscape.

The terrain must reflect a hillside mountain environment similar to Kakkadampoyil.

The terrain should include:

Elevation changes

Slopes

Retaining walls

Plateaus

Vegetation clusters

Natural contours

The terrain should be reconstructed from the architectural walkthrough video as closely as possible.

Terrain accuracy is more important than building accuracy.

The terrain determines the realism of the entire experience.

If necessary:

Use heightmaps.

Use sculpted terrain.

Use Blender terrain modeling.

Avoid procedural terrain generation without manual adjustments.
